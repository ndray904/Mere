To remove one thing, hold a finger on it. This works on any lantern, spring or koi.

  • Pebble. Tap the water to send out a ripple. Drag to skim a trail of them.
  • Lantern. Rings when a ripple reaches it. Higher on the water sings higher. Tap one to ring it yourself.
  • Spring. Drops a pebble on a steady pulse. Tap it to change the pulse.
  • Koi. Tap the water to release a fish. It wanders the pond and rings the lanterns it brushes past. Tap one to startle it.
  • Mood. The button at the top changes the key, the voice and the light.
